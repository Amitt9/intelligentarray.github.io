Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Refund Eligibility

Intelligent Array processes refunds for the following scenarios:

  • Transaction charged but service not delivered
  • Duplicate transaction / double charge
  • Technical error resulting in incorrect charge
  • Service cancelled before processing begins

Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days from the date of approval. The refund will be credited back to the original payment source — debit card, credit card, or bank account as applicable.

Non-Refundable Services

The following services are non-refundable once successfully processed:

  • Mobile recharges that have been activated
  • Utility bill payments that have been confirmed
  • Money transfers that have been completed and credited
  • Any service where the transaction has been successfully processed and confirmed

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us within 48 hours of the transaction at contact@intelligentarray.in with your transaction ID, date, amount, and reason for refund request. We will review and respond within 2 working days.

Cancellation Policy

Service requests may be cancelled before processing begins at no charge. Once a transaction has been initiated and is in processing state, cancellation may not be possible. Please contact us immediately if you wish to cancel a service request.
